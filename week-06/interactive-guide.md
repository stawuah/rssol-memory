# Week 06 Interactive Guide

## Big idea

This week is about doing many things at once without losing control.

## Explain like I'm five

Concurrency is many kids working on tasks at overlapping times.
Parallelism is many kids literally working at the same time.

## Distributed systems from scratch

Queues, timeouts, and retries are the daily bread of distributed systems.
If work arrives faster than it is processed, pressure builds.
That pressure is called backpressure.

## Checkpoints

- What is the difference between concurrency and parallelism?
- Why use channels instead of shared mutable state?
- What is backpressure?
- Why do timeouts create false positives sometimes?
