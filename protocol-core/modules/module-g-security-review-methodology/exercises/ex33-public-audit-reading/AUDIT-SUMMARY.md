# Public Audit Reading Assignment

Choose one real public Solana audit from Neodyme, OtterSec, or Sec3. Record report
title, URL, audited commit, publication date, scope, and auditor. Do not copy prose.

For three findings, write:

1. Finding ID/title and severity.
2. Protected asset and violated invariant.
3. Root cause in your own words.
4. Attacker prerequisites and exact exploit path.
5. User/protocol impact and why severity is justified.
6. Remediation and any residual risk.
7. A regression test you would add.
8. One lesson transferable to your capstone.

Finish by comparing the report's threat model with yours: what was out of scope,
which trust assumptions remained, and which issue would your existing tests miss?
