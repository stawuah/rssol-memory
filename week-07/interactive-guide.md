# Week 07 Interactive Guide

## Big idea

This week is about turning memory into durable state.

## Explain like I'm five

If you only remember things in your head, you lose them when you fall asleep.
Writing them in a notebook lets you recover later.

## Distributed systems from scratch

Durability means state survives crashes.
Logs are useful because they record what happened in order, and you can replay them.
