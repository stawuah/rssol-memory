# Week 10 Interactive Guide

## Big idea

This week is about safe interaction between programs.

## Explain like I'm five

If one kid asks another kid to move money from a piggy bank, both the request and the permission need to be checked carefully.

## Distributed systems from scratch

When one system calls another, you must validate assumptions at the boundary.
That is true for services and for Solana CPIs.

## Checkpoints

- What is a CPI?
- Why are owner checks and signer checks different?
- What is stale state?
- Why do negative tests matter as much as happy-path tests?
