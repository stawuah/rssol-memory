# Week 11 Interactive Guide

## Big idea

This week is about building the system around the program, not only the program itself.

## Explain like I'm five

The blockchain is the official notebook.
Your app still needs helpers to read it, summarize it, and show useful information to users.

## Distributed systems from scratch

Indexers and caches create eventual consistency.
Your UI may be briefly behind the chain even if nothing is broken.

## Checkpoints

- What is a read model?
- Why can indexers lag behind chain state?
- What happens if an event consumer misses an event?
- Where should truth live: cache or chain?
