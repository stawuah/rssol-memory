# 09 Confidential Transfers

## Analogy

Confidential transfer is tinted glass.

You can hide the amount, but not necessarily the time, path, or intent.

## Official facts

Solana’s confidential transfer docs say the extension transfers tokens without revealing the amount, but account addresses remain public. The flow includes mint setup, token account setup, deposit, apply pending balance, transfer, and withdraw.

- [Solana Confidential Transfer](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer)
