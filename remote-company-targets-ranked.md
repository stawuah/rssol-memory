# Remote Company Targets Ranked

This list is ranked for **your curriculum outcome**, not for prestige.

It is optimized for:

- remote or strongly remote-friendly roles
- startup / scale-up bias
- good compensation signals where publicly listed
- companies where Rust, Solana, protocol, or distributed-systems work is relevant

## How to read this

- `Highest probability`: Best fit after strong completion of the curriculum
- `Stretch`: Worth applying to if your projects are strong and polished
- `Senior stretch`: Apply selectively; these are realistic only if you also interview very well or already have substantial experience

## Important correction

Finishing the curriculum does **not** make you automatically senior.

What it does do:

- make you competitive for junior Rust roles
- make you competitive for early-to-mid Solana roles
- make you plausible for junior-to-mid protocol roles
- make some senior startup roles worth a shot if your project depth is exceptional

## How to aim by level

Apply aggressively to:

- highest-probability targets
- stretch targets with role levels from junior to mid

Apply selectively to:

- senior stretch targets
- staff or principal roles

Your strongest angle after this curriculum is:

- early-to-mid Solana engineer
- junior-to-mid protocol engineer
- junior-to-mid Rust/backend systems engineer

Your senior angle is only credible when:

- your capstone is excellent
- your explanations are sharp
- your GitHub is polished
- you already have strong adjacent professional experience
