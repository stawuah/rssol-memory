# Week 04 Interactive Guide

## Big idea

This week is about abstraction without losing control.

## Explain like I'm five

A trait is a promise about what something can do, even if different things do it differently.

## Distributed systems from scratch

Different storage backends can obey the same rules.
That is useful because systems often swap in-memory mocks for real storage during tests.

## Checkpoints

- What problem does a trait solve?
- Why are mocks useful in distributed systems?
- What should stay generic and what should stay concrete?
- When does abstraction make debugging harder?
